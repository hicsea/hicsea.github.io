University of Michigan - PhD Research

Ann Arbor, MI | August 2023 - Present

At the University of Michigan, I am researching how to make autonomous vehicles safe and reliable enough for real-world deployment. I am connecting software testing techniques with autonomous systems to find the rare, dangerous bugs that traditional testing misses. Finding these critical scenarios is essential to building trust with autonomous vehicles and making systems as robust as possible. I built systems that automatically run thousands of simulations to discover failures before they happen on real roads. I work closely with the Army Ground Vehicle Systems Center (GVSC) to help speed up the development of these systems by improving the testing process.

Founder

Journal Net

Ann Arbor, MI | January 2025 - Present

With Journal Net, I am connecting AI technology with proven therapeutic methods to make mental health tools actually useful. I built it because I am always trying to connect my internal value system to my actions, and I wanted to use my skills to provide a way for journal entries—something people already do—to be turned into a comprehensive network of your experiences, emotions, and beliefs. It ultimately shows how your values impact your life and lets you backtrack to understand how to make deep changes by tracing from feelings and experiences using IFS back to your belief system. This helps you figure out what parts of your belief system need to change, so you can have an internal conversation with your parts and make meaningful, lasting changes rather than just surface-level fixes.
