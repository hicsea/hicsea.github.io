Hi there! I'm a third-year PhD student in Naval Architecture & Marine Engineering at the University of Michigan, also pursuing an MSE in Computer Science & Engineering. I'm currently researching search-based test case generation methods for autonomous systems, working to automate test coverage in CI/CD pipelines with minimal human intervention to make these systems safer, more reliable, and more accessible.

## Research Focus

I'm developing novel unsupervised test generation methods that integrate fault injection parameters into genetic algorithm search spaces. My work involves designing extensible testing frameworks that integrate multiple simulation platforms and deploying these frameworks to production environments, including U.S. Army vehicle simulators. The goal is to discover critical edge cases faster than traditional fuzzing approaches while reducing the need for manual test case creation.

## What I'm Passionate About

I'm passionate about continuous growth, empowering teams, and using my skills to help people reach their potential. I care deeply about sustainability, mental and physical wellbeing, and creating solutions that drive positive change. Success for me is about improving people's lives through collaboration and high-functioning teams.

## Current Projects

### PhD Research
Search-based test case generation for autonomous systems - automating CI/CD test coverage to reduce costs while improving safety and reliability.

### Journal Net
Developing an AI-powered journaling platform that extends users' existing journal practice with minimal behavior change, using zero-knowledge encryption to ensure complete privacy of sensitive mental health data.

### Algorithmic Trading System
Leading a team building a full-stack experimentation platform with transformer-based time series models for quantitative trading strategies.

## Background

I previously worked as Chief Engineer for University of Michigan Electric Boat, where I led a 30-person engineering team building award-winning electric hydrofoil boats. I've interned at Herbert Engineering Corp. and Glosten, working on vessel design, optimization algorithms, and sustainable maritime transportation.

My work spans software engineering, machine learning, autonomous systems, and marine engineering - always with a focus on creating practical solutions to real-world problems.
