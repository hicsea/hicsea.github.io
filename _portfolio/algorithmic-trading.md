Leading algorithmic trading team as Scrum Master building full-stack experimentation platform for developing and testing quantitative strategies.

**Key Technologies:** FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL, PyTorch, Transformers

**Key Features:**
- Full-stack experimentation platform enabling development and testing of quantitative strategies benchmarked against buy-and-hold baselines
- Transformer-based time series models with configurable feature engineering and hyperparameter search
- Backtesting engine enabling rapid validation of trading strategies across historical data
- No-code React interface enabling domain experts to contribute without programming expertise

**Timeline:** May 2025 - Present
