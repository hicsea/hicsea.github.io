- Developed directed network design framework mapping offshore carbon sequestration variable interdependencies
- Designed floating carbon storage and injection unit from scratch including hullform optimization and general arrangements
- Conducted lifecycle cost analysis proving long-term profitability under Inflation Reduction Act incentives
- Presented at SNAME Maritime Convention 2023 with published paper
- Subsequent validation through commercial projects including MODEC's LCO2 FSIU and MISC/Samsung's FCSU
